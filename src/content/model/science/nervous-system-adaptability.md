## Why this construct

The nervous system must continually select, coordinate, suppress, and revise outputs as the body, task, environment, and perceived demands change. *Adaptability* refers to the available capacity for appropriate change, not simply the fact that an output changed.

## Important distinctions

Adaptability is not identical to variability, mobility, relaxation, or short-term performance. A highly stable strategy may be appropriate in one context, and variability without control may be dysfunctional. The construct must eventually include context, options, efficiency, and the ability to transition among strategies.

v0.1 uses a broad definition because the narrower operational definition has not yet been completed.
