## The current asymmetry

Nervous system adaptability was defined broadly because human adaptation includes motor, sensory, autonomic, and behavioral outputs. The operational model developed so far, however, relies chiefly on movement and muscle tone. Its most direct thrust evidence also concerns mechanoreceptive input and motor-system measures.

## Consequence

Changes in movement, cortical drive, spindle activity, or motor-unit behavior cannot be used as proxies for immune regulation, digestion, emotional regulation, neurodevelopment, disease prevention, or flourishing without additional causal and outcome evidence. Those domains may eventually become separate branches. v0.1 leaves them visibly unbuilt.
