## Consequence for analysis

Tone and movement can change with position, attention, load, fatigue, stress, expectation, pain, novelty, and perceived threat. A pattern may become visible only as demand approaches the person's available control.

In this model, *persistent* does not require an identical resting sign at every examination. It refers to a relatively stable bias or limited option set that reappears under relevant demands. That distinction is itself a hypothesis and needs longitudinal measurement.

Assessment should therefore explore how the system changes rather than merely catalogue resting asymmetry. It must also distinguish meaningful state dependence from noise, examiner influence, and normal within-person variability.
