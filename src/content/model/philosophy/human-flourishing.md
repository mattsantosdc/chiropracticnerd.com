## Why go beyond health

Health is valuable partly because of what it makes possible. People care about playing, learning, creating, relating, contributing, meeting challenges, and inhabiting their lives with greater freedom. This model uses *flourishing* as an umbrella for that wider horizon.

## Important restraint

Flourishing is plural and partly personal. It should not become a professional excuse to decide what another person's life ought to look like. Chiropractic can, at most, make a bounded contribution by supporting capacities relevant to living. Social conditions, relationships, meaning, resources, and many other influences remain outside its direct scope.
