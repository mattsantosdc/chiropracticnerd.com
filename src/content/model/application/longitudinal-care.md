## Why the question arises

Motor strategies and demands change over time, and an immediate response may not persist. That makes reassessment over time conceptually reasonable. It does not tell us when to reassess or whether another input is beneficial.

## v0.1 boundary

Frequency should not be derived from philosophy, habit, membership structure, or the assumption that everyone always has a subluxation. Future entries should separate short-term corrective experiments, episodic reassessment, and ongoing elective care, then test the outcomes and tradeoffs of each.
