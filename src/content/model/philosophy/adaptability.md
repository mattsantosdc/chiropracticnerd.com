## Why this claim is present

Living beings continually encounter changing internal and external demands. The ability to adjust while maintaining or developing viable function is therefore a useful starting point for asking what supports life, health, and the possibilities available to a person.

The Model adopts adaptability as a visible premise, not as a conclusion deduced from a more elaborate chiropractic doctrine. Its biological component is open to empirical study, while the importance assigned to health and human potential also contains an evaluative choice. Those parts should not be defended as though they were the same kind of claim.

## Boundary

Calling adaptability fundamental does not make it a complete definition of life or health, and greater adaptability is not automatically better in every context. The relevant changes must remain appropriate to the person, demand, and timescale.
