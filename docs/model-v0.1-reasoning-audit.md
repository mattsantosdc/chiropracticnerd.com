# Model v0.1 reasoning audit

This audit evaluates M-004–M-006, M-007–M-013, and M-014–M-016 as written on 2026-09-09, alongside all four structured arguments. With the three Framework entries, the Model contains 16 entries and 23 direct revision dependencies. Claims and inferential roles were inspected before revising empirical proposals. Dependencies record revision impact, not reasoning or evidence. The original structural revision added no evidence review. The focused Science revision below added limited research examples for M-008 and M-009, without claiming comprehensive evidential appraisal or chiropractic confirmation. The subsequent M-012/M-013 split and Current evidence sections clarify the existing proposals and appraisal without adding empirical evidence or changing confidence.

## Overall finding

Open-ended human functional potential is the philosophical starting point. Net-positive functional improvement is the explicit valued aim, not a synonym for change or adaptability. Better function is itself a benefit within this Model, without requiring an additional identified consequence or attribution of that consequence to the improvement. Whether improvement occurred, whether chiropractic caused it, and whether it brings a specific downstream benefit remain separate empirical questions. M-006 permits a rationale without symptoms or pathology; it does not demonstrate effectiveness in asymptomatic people. M-005's boundary distinguishes a hoped-for symptomatic outcome from the Model's proposed functional action of chiropractic. M-006 and M-014 now distinguish an adjustment's functional indication from symptoms and pathology considered for safety and comfort. ARG-002 exposes the proposed target requirement without treating it as a deduction from the philosophical rationale. Measurement, detection, and effectiveness remain unresolved.

Science distinguishes a state-dependent neuromotor domain, modifiable opportunities, general perturbation, beneficial chiropractic effects, broader benefit, and the separate proposal of principal neural mediation. These are proposed truths with confidence assessed separately; none silently establishes the next. M-012 concerns net-positive benefit; M-013 concerns broader functional effects whose value may remain unresolved. Art preserves assessment → conditional input → reassessment, with non-intervention and stopping available. The stronger hypothesis that all people may retain chiropractic benefit potential across changing states is compatible with this structure, but is neither required nor established.

All arguments remain defeasible. No scientific conclusion is presented as deductively established, and no empirical premise was created to complete a desired chain. The open assumptions and bridges below limit what may be inferred without blocking an inspectable v0.1.

## Terminology boundaries

| Concept | Role | Does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open-ended functional potential | Philosophical starting point across changing circumstances | Unlimited capacity, a measurable imperfection now, or chiropractic responsiveness |

| Neuromotor constraint | Working description of a current motor-control pattern that limits functional options relative to postural or task demands | Pathology, a specific CNS pathway, validated detection, or benefit from changing it |
| Opportunity for beneficial neuromotor change | Empirical possibility that changing a candidate pattern improves function overall | Universal prevalence, practical availability, or chiropractic responsiveness |
| Neurobiomechanical perturbation | General possible mechanism of reevaluation and reorganization | Intentional training, successful reorganization, or benefit |
| Neurobiomechanical training stimulus | Intentional chiropractic use of an input | Learning, retention, causal effect, or fulfilled intention |
| Actionable chiropractic opportunity | Practical working judgment of sufficient expected value from an available input now | Biological perfection when none is identified, or validated assessment capability |
| Broader functional benefit | Downstream empirical outcome question | A consequence that follows from an immediate neuromotor response |
| Principal neural mediation | Empirical proposal about the main route to broader chiropractic effects | Benefit, an exclusively neural effect, or identification of a particular pathway |

## Focused Philosophy audit after wording revisions

Scope: review M-004–M-006 as currently written, including removal of the deferred universal-benefit hypothesis sentence from M-006, clarification of M-005's symptom wording, and the subsequent distinction between adjustment indication and safety or comfort. The latter clarification also updates M-014, ARG-002, and ARG-004. Their main claims, premise sets, and conclusions remain unchanged. M-016's detailed treatment of new or worsening symptoms remains expressly deferred.

### What holds together

The three main claims provide an inspectable philosophical foundation: human functional potential is open-ended; net-positive functional improvement is a legitimate chiropractic aim; symptoms and pathology are not required to supply that rationale. They do not establish that a particular person needs an adjustment or that a particular input produces improvement.

M-004 is adequately bounded as a framework commitment. Its explanation distinguishes open-ended possibilities across circumstances from unlimited capacity, inevitable progress, or a presently detectable imperfection in everyone. No additional philosophical premise is needed simply to retain this starting point.

M-005 explicitly values improved function itself. A separately identified symptom or health outcome is unnecessary to establish that value. This does not establish that a particular observation represents improvement or that chiropractic caused it. Keeping the entry classified as a value claim preserves that distinction.

M-006's main scope claim is coherent with the stated meanings of M-004 and M-005. ARG-004 exposes the interpretation that an aim legitimate in its own right can supply an independent rationale. Its defeasible classification remains appropriate: the brief premise claims alone do not force every interpretation of their scope, and no efficacy conclusion follows.

### Findings and current status

1. **M-005's symptom wording is clarified.** The boundary now describes the proposed functional action of chiropractic rather than presenting functional and symptomatic purposes as alternative bases for intervention. A person or chiropractor may hope for symptom relief, but that hope changes neither the proposed physiological action nor the functional grounds for indication and selection. Actual net-positive functional improvement remains a successful functional outcome even without the hoped-for symptom change. The claim remains a value claim about a legitimate aim; its boundary previews the Model's empirical account without establishing that account from the value premise. Indicator validity remains unresolved; the separate reasoning about adjustment indication is addressed below.

2. **The indication principle now distinguishes the adjustment target from safety and comfort.** M-006 replaces the ambiguous “neither constitute nor contribute” wording with the narrower statement that symptoms and pathology are not themselves indicators for an adjustment. The intended reasoning is explicit in M-014 and ARG-002: within the proposed account, adjustment targets a neuromotor constraint with an opportunity for beneficial change through an available input; a symptom or diagnosis does not itself establish that target or opportunity. If a person has pain or a medical problem without the relevant constraint, that proposed adjustment has no target. Symptoms and pathology may nevertheless require withholding or modifying an input for safety and comfort. This target requirement is part of the proposed practical account, not a deduction from M-004 and M-005 or an assertion that symptoms can never be related to neuromotor function. ARG-004 continues to support only the philosophical rationale beyond symptoms. The clarification resolves the ambiguity and exposes the conditional reasoning; it does not validate the target construct, detection, selection, or mechanism. Failure to identify an opportunity is not proof of its absence, and unsuccessful inputs cannot be excluded from evaluation by retrospectively relabeling them. No new premise or Model entry is introduced.

3. **“Net-positive” remains an acknowledged evaluative and measurement gap.** M-005 states the aim but supplies no complete criterion for deciding which changes count as better function or how gains and losses are weighed. That is acceptable for the philosophical statement of an aim at v0.1, but not sufficient to certify clinical success. A person's hoped-for outcome must not redefine what an indicator measures; goals and preferences can still inform the value of pursuing care and its burdens. The distinction between measured change, functional improvement, net benefit, and a further symptomatic outcome must survive future operationalization.

## Focused Science audit after wording revisions

Scope of that earlier revision: retain M-007; clarify M-008's motor domain and give M-009's proposed target a concrete working description. IDs, slugs, claim types, then-current status labels, and unresolved confidence were preserved. The later removal of editorial status is recorded below.

### Reasoning before evidence

M-008 asserts variation with the person's state, task, and environment, together with possible limitations in available or suitable motor strategies. The domain includes the neural regulation of muscle tone, posture, and movement. Resting tone is a potential assessment finding; it does not independently identify a motor strategy or its neural mechanism.

M-009 concerns motor strategies that limit functional options in a given context. Its body defines a **neuromotor constraint** as the current pattern, separately from the opportunity for worthwhile change. The main statement remains empirical: some such strategies can be modified with overall functional benefit. The working definition does not demonstrate that the described pattern exists or that an assessment detects it.

The proposed route requires independently assessable steps: a pattern limits useful options under specified conditions; it is modifiable under specified inputs and timescales; and the change produces functional gains worth its relevant costs. M-008 alone establishes neither the latter two steps nor a person-level finding. M-005 supplies the meaning of overall improvement, not evidence that it occurs. No premise or deductive argument is added to fill these gaps.

The co-contraction example is illustrative, with no specific CNS pathway asserted. The subluxation paragraph identifies a current motor-control state as the proposed target of assessment and adjustment without assuming displacement, tissue pathology, or disease causation. It asserts no equivalence to all professional uses of the term and adds no historical source as empirical authority.

### Limited empirical check

## Unresolved reasoning and empirical bridges

These are requirements for stronger claims, not accepted new Model entries.

| Bridge or assumption | What is still needed | Inference currently withheld |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Functional potential → chiropractic benefit | A relevant opportunity, an effective input in the person and state concerned, and net-positive outcomes | Philosophy cannot show that an asymptomatic person, every person, or this person now benefits. |

| Neural integration → principal chiropractic pathway | Evidence distinguishing neural mediation from competing explanations, with temporal and causal specificity | M-007 does not establish M-013's neural mediation or its primacy for chiropractic. |
| Within-cycle response → justified subsequent input | Reliable reassessment, valid decision thresholds, and sufficient expected value for the next input | Neither change nor lack of change makes repeated input obligatory. |

M-012 now **states** the unresolved chiropractic-to-broader-function hypothesis instead of leaving it implicit between generic adaptability claims. This improves inspectability; it supplies no evidence. The former M-011/M-012 wording is no longer the audit's object. Its general lesson survives: possibilities whose cases and conditions need not overlap do not form a deductive causal chain. M-009 and M-011 do not establish a present match, and neither establishes M-012.

Mechanism failure requires revising that explanation, not declaring every possible effect impossible. A broader effect without neural mediation would not validate the proposed pathway. Null findings support absence only within a scope where valid measurement, adequate sensitivity and power, and suitable comparisons could detect the specified effect; weak or mismatched tests establish only failure to detect it.
