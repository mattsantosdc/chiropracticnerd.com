## Why “may” matters

Improved adaptability is defined as improved capacity to change outputs appropriately. It therefore has conceptual relevance to function. That does not establish the size, duration, domains, or practical importance of any effect produced by chiropractic care.

The claim also does not imply that every adjustment improves whole-person health, prevents disease, enhances development, or produces flourishing. Those are distinct outcomes requiring their own evidence.
