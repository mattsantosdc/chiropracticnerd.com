[The philosophy, science, and art framework](/model/framework/philosophy-science-art/) assigns distinct, connected work to philosophy, science, and art. [The claim-support principle](/model/framework/claim-discipline/) requires logical validity, premise truth, evidential strength, and statement type to remain distinguishable. Together they motivate [the reasoning and empirical-testing method](/model/framework/explicit-reasoning-empirical-testing/): expose the proposed inference, then evaluate empirical premises independently and propagate warranted revisions.

This is a methodological synthesis, not a deductive proof that [the reasoning and empirical-testing method](/model/framework/explicit-reasoning-empirical-testing/) is the only possible authoring method. Its conclusion remains a revisable framework commitment.
