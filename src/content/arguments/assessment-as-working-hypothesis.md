## Inferential route

P-002 supplies the legitimate aim of net-positive functional improvement. P-003 makes that rationale available without requiring symptoms or pathology. S-003 proposes that neuromotor function can present state-dependent, modifiable opportunities for net-positive change. S-005 proposes intentional chiropractic use of neurobiomechanical training stimuli while leaving their mechanism and beneficial effects empirical.

The practical reasoning scheme is conditional: when pursuing a legitimate aim through an uncertain intervention, identify the relevant present opportunity, assess whether any available input has sufficient expected value, and state the prediction as a revisable working hypothesis. This decision principle is the argument's normative bridge; it is not a causal fact derived from S-003 or S-005. It motivates A-001's assessment strategy and its “what, if any, input” condition, without requiring action.

## Scope and unresolved premises

The scientific possibilities need not coincide in the same person, state, task, input, or timescale. S-003 does not establish that an opportunity is detectable or responsive to chiropractic. S-005's intended beneficial alteration does not establish successful alteration or a validated rule for choosing the most promising input. P-003 permits a rationale without symptoms; it supplies no efficacy evidence in asymptomatic people.

A warranted decision to proceed would additionally require sufficiently reliable observations, a credible match between the proposed opportunity and available input, and a benefit-risk judgment that accounts for the person's goals, preferences, safety, alternatives, and context. Those person-level conditions remain to be established; they are not accepted empirical premises hidden in this argument. An observed neuromotor response would also be insufficient to justify broader benefit.

The conclusion is therefore a defeasible strategy for inquiry and selection, not proof that the assessment works or that an actionable opportunity exists. No actionable opportunity identified is an admissible result. ARG-002 retains its identity as the practical rationale for assessment as a working hypothesis, but its route now concerns opportunities and conditional selection rather than detection of a defined entity.
