M-001 assigns distinct, connected work to philosophy, science, and art. M-002 requires logical validity, premise truth, evidential strength, and claim type to remain distinguishable. Together they motivate the authoring method in M-003: expose the proposed inference, then evaluate empirical premises independently and propagate warranted revisions.

This is a methodological synthesis, not a deductive proof that M-003 is the only possible authoring method. Its conclusion remains a revisable framework commitment.
