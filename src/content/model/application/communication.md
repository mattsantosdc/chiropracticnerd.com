## Practical standard

The chiropractor can explain that the examination is looking for patterns of constrained movement and tone, that the proposed input is intended to influence sensorimotor organization, and that response will be reassessed. The explanation should not turn a mechanistic hypothesis into a diagnosis or promise disease prevention, developmental enhancement, or whole-person transformation.

Symptoms and conditions may be discussed honestly as context and outcomes without redefining chiropractic as their treatment. Uncertainty is not a failure of communication. It is part of informed participation in a developing model.
