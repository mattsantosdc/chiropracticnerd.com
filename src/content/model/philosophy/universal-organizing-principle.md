## Historical continuity

Stephenson's Major Premise said that Universal Intelligence is in all matter and continually gives matter its properties and actions. That language remains recognizable to many chiropractors, but the word *intelligence* commonly implies mind, intention, or an entity. This version preserves the observation that reality is lawful and organized while declining to infer those additional properties.

## What this does not establish

This entry does not identify a being, force, plan, moral order, or purpose behind nature. It does not prove that every observed arrangement is optimal. It also does not explain biological organization by itself. Those are separate claims.

## Open question

“Universal Organizing Principle” is a working label. The model still needs to decide whether it adds explanatory value beyond *natural regularity*, whether *organization* has been defined tightly enough, and whether any part of the historical concept should remain metaphysically open rather than naturalistically translated.
