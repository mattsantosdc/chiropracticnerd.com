## Purpose before mechanism

This entry identifies what chiropractic ought to be for. It intentionally does not yet claim how chiropractic accomplishes that purpose. The science layer must identify a tractable target and test whether the art can influence it.

## A bounded contribution

Chiropractic is not a complete theory of health or a route to every component of flourishing. Its proposed contribution is narrower: influence aspects of embodied adaptability and function through a distinctive assessment and adjusting practice. The Neuroadaptive Model attempts to make that mechanism specific enough to investigate.
