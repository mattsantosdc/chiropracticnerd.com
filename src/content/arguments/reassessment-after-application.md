A-001 frames assessment as a working hypothesis, and A-002 applies a selected input intended to test that hypothesis in practice. If the strategy is to learn from that intervention rather than merely deliver it, repeating the relevant assessment provides information for retaining, revising, or abandoning the working hypothesis and for deciding what follows.

This is practical, defeasible reasoning rather than deduction. The premises do not establish that the reassessment is reliable, that an observed change was caused by the input, or that an immediate response predicts a durable or broader benefit. Those empirical questions require separate evidence.
