## Actual benefit as a conclusion

This statement combines an empirical commitment with an evaluative one. S-011 asserts that some inputs cause reorganization that improves context-appropriate function. S-022 supplies the value of that same improvement. ARG-007 records the deductive inference to a corresponding functional benefit. The empirical claim remains indispensable even though the inference is deductive.

“Some” asserts occurrence without specifying frequency, population prevalence, predictable selection, or benefit for everyone. The same input-produced reorganization must provide the improvement: separate observations that some inputs reorganize function and some reorganizations improve it do not establish that overlap.

## Current evidence

Confidence remains unresolved because the joint causal improvement claim in S-011 has not been established by the evidence documented in this account. General motor adaptation, successful-adjustment terminology, and the value premise supply no missing chiropractic effect evidence. This is an adopted working conclusion, with empirical support evaluated separately from the validity of ARG-007.

## Boundary

The conclusion concerns the specified neuromotor benefit. It does not establish broader transfer, the perturbation mechanism, principal neural mediation of broader effects, duration beyond the evaluated interval, or a decision to pursue an input. Those questions remain distinct even if this conclusion is true.
