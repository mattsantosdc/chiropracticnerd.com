## Why this claim is present

Assessment becomes action only when its working hypothesis is translated into a particular input. Force application is that practical step: an input is selected and delivered with the intention of perturbing the motor strategy judged to be constrained and inviting a more adaptive response.

The dependency on A-001 carries the hypothesis and its proposed selection into action. Through A-001's own dependencies, this strategy is also constrained by the definition of subluxation and the unresolved empirical claim that appropriately selected chiropractic inputs can increase neuromotor adaptability.

## Boundary

Intention does not establish effect. Delivering force—even when it matches the working hypothesis—does not prove that the intended strategy was perturbed or that reorganization occurred. v0.1 does not specify contact, direction, magnitude, rate, duration, dose, or a universal form of input. Those choices must later be made explicit and tested rather than inferred from success.
