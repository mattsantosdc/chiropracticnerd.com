## Why this claim is present

This entry gives the purpose in P-002 a boundary. Within this Model, chiropractic is defined by an affirmative aim—enhancing adaptability in service of human potential—rather than by the conditions or complaints a person may have.

The boundary is a normative choice about chiropractic's identity, not a deduction from adaptability and not an empirical conclusion that symptoms, injuries, or disease are irrelevant. Those concerns remain important to a person's context, safety, decisions, and use of other forms of care. Changes in them may also be observed without becoming chiropractic's defining purpose.

## Boundary

The word “treatment” can refer to an ultimate professional purpose or to actions and outcomes that occur within lawful practice. This entry addresses the defining purpose. A fuller account will need to keep that distinction clear rather than using the boundary to dismiss a person's concerns or overstate chiropractic's scope.
