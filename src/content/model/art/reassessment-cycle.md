## Why this claim is present

Repeating the relevant assessment after force application turns the art into an iterative learning process. The post-application findings may support the working hypothesis, fail to support it, or suggest that the pattern or selected input was misunderstood. They then guide the decision to stop, revise the hypothesis, or consider another input.

Conceptually, the result feeds into the next A-001 cycle. It is represented here in prose rather than as an `A-003 → A-001` upstream dependency, because that arrow would create a cycle and misstate an iterative cross-link as a same-cycle dependency.

## Boundary

Within-cycle reassessment is a provisional test, not proof that the input caused the observed change. Natural variability, repeated testing, expectation, measurement error, and other contextual influences remain alternatives. Immediate findings also do not establish durability or broader function; the assessment methods and decision thresholds still require independent evaluation.
