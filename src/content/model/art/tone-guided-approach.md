## Historical bridge

D. D. Palmer described tone as foundational to chiropractic and related muscular and organic tension to nervous-system tonicity. The present model does not simply restore that historical theory, but it recognizes an important continuity: chiropractic has long been concerned with regulated tension and function, not only bone position.

## Current working application

The examination compares tone at rest and during movement, especially as a person approaches the position or task where the pattern becomes less adaptable. The current strategy explores a provocative window near the onset of the constrained response and often positions the implicated hypertonic action under lengthening demand before the input.

The exact directional rule, receptor-level explanation, and relationship between hypertonic and hypotonic sides remain unsettled. They are intentionally not promoted to foundational claims in v0.1.
