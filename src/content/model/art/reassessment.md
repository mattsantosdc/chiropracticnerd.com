## A within-visit experiment

The pre-intervention finding supplies a prediction. A matched input is delivered. The same or a closely related task is then repeated. The response may support the hypothesis, fail to support it, or reveal a different pattern.

Immediate change is not proof of lasting benefit, and lack of immediate change does not identify the reason for failure. Reassessment is still useful because it prevents technical completion from being confused with demonstrated response and gives the art a disciplined feedback loop.
