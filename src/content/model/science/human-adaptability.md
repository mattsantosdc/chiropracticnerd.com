## Why this claim is present

P-001 introduces adaptability broadly. S-001 begins the empirical layer by identifying one proposed contributor that can be studied more specifically: the nervous system's capacity to update and coordinate responses when internal conditions, environments, or tasks change.

The conceptual edge from P-001 narrows the question; it does not supply evidence for the answer. Whether nervous-system updating contributes to human adaptability, how much it contributes, and how that contribution should be measured are empirical matters to be tested against reality.

## Boundary

“Depends in part” is essential. Human adaptability also reflects bodily structures, metabolic and immune processes, learning, relationships, environments, resources, and many other influences. This entry neither makes the nervous system the sole controller of the person nor claims that every nervous-system change is adaptive.
