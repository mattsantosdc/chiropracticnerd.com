## Proposed sequence

1. Define adaptability, motor strategy, persistence, and meaningful change with enough precision to measure them.
2. Establish whether the proposed examination findings are reliable within and across examiners and stable enough to interpret.
3. Test whether findings distinguish predicted states from normal variation, structural constraint, pathology, pain adaptation, and examiner expectation.
4. Test whether intervention selected from the findings changes the predicted measures more than credible controls and alternative inputs.
5. Determine duration, dose-response, moderators, and the relationship between immediate changes and meaningful function.
6. Only then test longitudinal, symptom-independent, developmental, health, and flourishing claims at the appropriate scale.

Disconfirming results should revise the graph at the failed dependency rather than be absorbed through a broader definition.
