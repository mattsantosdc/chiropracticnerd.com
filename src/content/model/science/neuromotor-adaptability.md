## Why this claim is present

The broad construct in S-001 needs observable expressions if it is to guide research or practice. Neuromotor adaptability focuses attention on how movement responses update, coordinate, and vary as the person, task, or environment changes.

This entry identifies an expression of nervous-system adaptability; it does not define a complete measurement system. Candidate observations might eventually concern available strategies, coordination, or context-sensitive change, but v0.1 does not promote any specific finding or test to core status.

## Boundary

Neuromotor adaptability is one expression, not a proxy for the whole nervous system or the whole person. A measured movement change may reflect learning, effort, expectation, fatigue, pain, tissue properties, or measurement error. Reliability and validity must be established before an observation can support a claim about adaptability.
