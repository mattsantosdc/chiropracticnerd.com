## Why this is the framework

This meta-layer does not make Philosophy, Science, and Art three independent departments that can generate incompatible versions of chiropractic. They perform different, connected jobs.

Philosophy asks what kind of phenomenon chiropractic is concerned with, what health means, and what ends chiropractic should pursue. Science constrains every empirical answer and supplies increasingly specific explanations. Art is the skilled, contextual application of that knowledge. Practical experience generates new questions and exposes weak assumptions, so information also flows back upstream.

## Epistemic orientation

**Guided by first principles. Tested against reality.**

First principles help make the Model's assumptions visible, coherent, and traceable. They do not deduce chiropractic upward or settle empirical questions. Claims about mechanisms, observations, and effects remain answerable to evidence, and results from research and practice can require revisions anywhere in the Model.

## Historical continuity

Chiropractors already recognize the profession as a philosophy, science, and art. This entry keeps that inherited structure while making the relationship more explicit. It also rejects the idea that a philosophical premise may settle an empirical question without evidence.

## v0.1 boundary

The ordering is a working methodological commitment, not a claim that the historical profession always used these words consistently. The model should revise this entry if the sequence obscures a better account of how values, evidence, and skilled practice interact.
