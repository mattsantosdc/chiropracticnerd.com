## Candidate information

v0.1 includes active and passive movement, available range, movement quality, regional and side-to-side tone, task-dependent changes, protective responses, and the effect of altered position or load. History and symptoms provide context and may suggest states worth testing, but they do not define the target.

## A hypothesis, not a verdict

The examination produces a working hypothesis about a constrained strategy. Structural limitation, injury, inflammation, development, pain adaptation, neurologic disease, habit, and normal variation remain alternatives. No validated diagnostic rule presently converts these observations into a confirmed Neuroadaptive subluxation.
