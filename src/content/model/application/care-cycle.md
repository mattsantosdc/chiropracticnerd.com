## The v0.1 cycle

1. Understand the person's goals, relevant history, current state, preferences, and safety context.
2. Explore movement and tone across appropriate positions or tasks.
3. Form a limited hypothesis about a region and constrained strategy, including reasonable alternatives.
4. Decide whether an input is suitable and select its form and parameters.
5. Deliver the input with consent.
6. Reassess the predicted pattern and note unexpected changes.
7. Stop, modify, refer, or continue based on the response and the person's context.

The cycle is deliberately technique-agnostic. A named method is compatible only if its decisions can be traced through these constraints.
