## Exact route

S-022 states: “Any change that improves context-appropriate human function within a specified functional scope, context, and timescale provides a functional benefit within that scope, context, and timescale.”

S-024 states: “An adjustment is neuromotor reorganization caused by a chiropractic input, where reorganization means a change in the organization of neuromotor control or coordination.”

S-011 states: “Some chiropractic inputs cause neuromotor reorganization that improves context-appropriate human function within a specified neuromotor scope, context, and timescale.”

Therefore S-027: “Some chiropractic inputs cause neuromotor reorganization that provides a functional benefit within a specified neuromotor scope, context, and timescale.”

Assume S-011 true and take one of its input-produced reorganizations. S-024 defines reorganization as a change in neuromotor control or coordination. That very change improves function under S-011. S-022 attributes functional benefit to it within the same scope, context, and timescale. The input causation and existential quantifier are retained; the conclusion adds neither broader transfer nor a new causal link. A neuromotor scope is a functional scope, so the value premise applies without changing domains of outcome.

## What remains empirical

S-011 must be true for this route to establish actual benefit. Its unresolved confidence is not improved by the deduction. S-009's general modifiability and S-020's sensory-updating capacity cannot replace the joint causal premise: their cases need not overlap with chiropractic inputs that produce improving reorganization.

The success definition and S-026 are unnecessary premises here. Defining success supplies no occurrence evidence. Likewise, S-028's perturbation explanation is a separate empirical commitment; the benefit inference can remain valid if another mechanism explains S-011's effect.

None of the premises supplies a success frequency, prospective selection rule, reliable assessment, durable change beyond the specified interval, broader benefit, principal neural mediation, or sufficient justification to proceed with an intervention. ARG-005 and the practical arguments remain defeasible. The conclusion preserves S-022's functional-benefit scope throughout; it makes no judgment about the intervention's worth after all costs, risks, and alternatives are considered.
