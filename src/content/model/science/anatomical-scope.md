## Why this matters

Historically, chiropractic subluxation has been vertebral, even when its proposed effects extended beyond the involved joint. The Neuroadaptive definition describes a constrained motor strategy associated with a *region*. Similar regulated constraints could plausibly be expressed around an extremity, the jaw, or another area, but plausibility does not settle whether each should be called a chiropractic subluxation.

## v0.1 boundary

The current art is predominantly spine-centered and gives the upper cervical region special working attention. The Model should not silently expand the construct to every joint or muscle. A later decision must distinguish the scientific possibility of nonspinal constrained strategies from the chosen domain and terminology of chiropractic.
