## Why the placeholder is necessary

In the historical framework, force linked intelligence and matter. In contemporary use, *force* has a technical physical meaning, while chiropractors may also use it to mean causal influence, an adjustment input, vital activity, or information. Moving among those meanings without notice creates equivocation.

## v0.1 rule

Mechanical force describes a physical input. Neural information describes structured differences in activity interpreted within a biological system. Neither term by itself supplies intention, life, health, or organization. The Model will not use an undefined vital force or information metaphor to bridge a missing mechanism.
