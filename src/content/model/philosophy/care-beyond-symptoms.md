## Why this claim is present

P-001 declines to treat normality or good health as an endpoint to functional potential. P-002 adopts net-positive functional improvement as a legitimate aim in its own right. On that basis, the Model does not require symptoms, injury, disease, or identifiable pathology to supply the rationale for considering chiropractic care. The structured route and its limits are recorded in ARG-004.

## Boundary

A functional rationale for chiropractic care does not, by itself, establish that a particular input will improve function or that proceeding is justified for a particular person.

The indication for an adjustment rests on a working assessment of an opportunity for beneficial neuromotor change, as described in A-001. Symptoms, injury, disease, and identifiable pathology neither constitute nor contribute to that indication. They may require withholding an adjustment or modifying how it is delivered for safety and comfort. A person's hoped-for symptomatic outcome does not change the functional basis for selecting the input.

Nothing here establishes that everyone always needs or can benefit from an adjustment.
