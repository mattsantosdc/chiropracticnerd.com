## More than a named technique

The art includes what to observe, how to create a useful demand, whether an intervention is suitable, where and how to deliver it, how much input to use, what to reassess, and how to learn from the response. A named technique may organize some of those decisions, but no technique is exempt from the upstream model.

## Constraint and judgment

Person-specific judgment is necessary because bodies, states, goals, risks, and responses differ. Judgment does not mean intuition is automatically correct. The art should make its decision rules visible enough to teach, compare, test, and revise.
