## Why this claim is present

S-001 proposes that nervous-system updating and coordination contribute to human adaptability. S-006 states a related empirical hypothesis: changing nervous-system adaptability can influence how a person adapts and functions more broadly.

This is a causal possibility, not a claim about the size, duration, or importance of any particular effect. “Can influence” must eventually be replaced by more specific predictions about which nervous-system changes affect which dimensions of function, under what conditions, and for how long.

## Boundary

No dependency from S-005 is encoded. The current skeleton does not establish that a chiropractic-produced change in neuromotor adaptability is the kind, magnitude, or duration of nervous-system change that carries into broader human adaptability. That bridge remains an unresolved structural question rather than an implied conclusion.
