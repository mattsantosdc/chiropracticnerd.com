## What is preserved

The historical concept points toward something real and important: living organisms actively coordinate vast numbers of processes without conscious direction. They develop, regulate, repair, and adapt as integrated wholes.

## What remains unresolved

Calling those capacities “Innate Intelligence” can suggest a separate agent inside the organism. Replacing the phrase with *self-regulation* may be too narrow, while using it for every biological process risks making it unfalsifiable and redundant.

v0.1 therefore keeps the historical bridge but does not make this entry carry downstream scientific conclusions. The next pass should clarify whether it names one general organizational property, a family of biological capacities, or primarily a philosophical perspective.
