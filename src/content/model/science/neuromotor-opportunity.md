## Why this claim is present

S-003 proposes that some neuromotor states permit changes that would be beneficial overall in the person's context. A limitation in available or context-appropriate strategies may be a candidate, but S-002 does not establish that a particular limitation can or should be changed. Benefit must be evaluated using explicit outcomes and tradeoffs, consistent with P-002.

This is an unresolved empirical possibility, not a definition that makes opportunities exist. Modifiability must be demonstrated for specified states, inputs, and timescales; it does not imply modifiability by chiropractic. A potentially beneficial change also need not be detectable or practically available now.

## Boundary

An opportunity is not a new named lesion or a distinct biological entity. The entry makes no prevalence claim and does not assert an opportunity in every person at every instant. “Actionable chiropractic opportunity” belongs to A-001's practical judgment about what, if anything, can presently be justified.

The Model no longer needs a canonical definition of subluxation to pose this scientific question. Historical and contemporary uses of that term remain subjects for articles and analysis; they do not establish the present hypothesis. S-003 retains its role as the proposed scientific access point while replacing the former definition with a testable possibility claim.
