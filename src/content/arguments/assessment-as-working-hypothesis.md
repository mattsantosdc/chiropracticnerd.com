P-002 supplies the adaptability-oriented purpose, S-003 supplies the proposed meaning of subluxation, and S-005 supplies the provisional possibility that a selected chiropractic input can increase neuromotor adaptability. Taken together, they motivate assessment as a person-specific working hypothesis about whether the defined pattern may be present and whether an input may be appropriate.

This route is defeasible. S-003 does not establish that subluxation exists or can be detected, and S-005 does not supply a validated person-level selection rule. Safety, preferences, measurement validity, alternatives, and context can defeat a decision to proceed even if these premises are retained. The premises therefore motivate the form of the strategy; they do not prove that an assessment finding is correct.
