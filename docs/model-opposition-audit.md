# Substantive opposition audit

This audit examines the working Model at the completed relationship migration: 32 statements and ten arguments. It assesses the strongest identifiable challenges to the account's premises, interpretations, inference schemes and practical conditions. It is an audit of the stated reasoning and its existing evidence boundaries, not a new literature appraisal or a declaration that every possible objection has been answered.

The result is conditional coherence with substantive open questions. The scoped causal effect, mechanism, broader transfer, measurement and selection commitments remain empirical burdens. Formal acceptance in the unopposed working theory does not resolve them. No new empirical claim or confidence upgrade is introduced by this audit.

All exact propositions and the three strict inferences retain their prior commitments. The audit adds six public questions, Q-019 through Q-024, to the existing 18. The questions are charitable challenges to this account, not assertions about other practitioners or automatic counterpremises.

## Executable conditional challenges

`reasoning/opposition-scenarios.json` contains eight explicitly hypothetical, versioned scenarios. Each names the exact targets and critical questions, premise removals or additions, any separately stated hypothetical undercutter, and the expected interpretation of support. `reasoning/opposition.py` validates that contract, creates an isolated full working-theory copy, and supplies no implicit premises. `reasoning:pilot` evaluates every scenario and checks AIF round trips before writing reports.

| Scenario | Assumed change | What the evaluation checks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OP-001 | Withdraw both S-011 and S-028 premise routes. | The effect/benefit/purpose/application routes lose support without being negated; independent broader premises are not silently withdrawn. |
| OP-002 | Add the exact negation of S-011. | The positive effect and mechanism support become unresolved; additional arguments cannot outvote the contradiction. |
| OP-003 | Replace S-028 with its negation. | Independent S-011, assessment and application remain supported; no replacement mechanism is inferred. |
| OP-004 | Assume an overriding responsibility defeats ARG-008's use of its normative bridge. | Aim and dependent strategy arguments lose accepted support while scoped benefit remains supported; the aim is not automatically negated. |
| OP-005 | Withdraw S-022. | Effect support survives while these value, purpose and application routes lose support. |
| OP-006 | Withdraw S-032. | Assessment survives while the application and associated reassessment routes lose support. |
| OP-007 | Assume a defeater of ARG-010's general practical inference. | Defeat propagates through application to reassessment without asserting their negations. The scenario stipulates a defeater; it does not mistake one unmet case condition for a refutation of the strategy. |
| OP-008 | Withdraw S-012, S-013 and S-031. | The local benefit, purpose and practical strategy remain independently supported. |

OP-004 and OP-007 exercise declared undercuts. They deliberately do not claim to have established their hypothetical grounds. An actual admitted objection requires its own proposition, role, evidence or normative justification and exact target. Empirical alternatives such as measurement error in some cases are compatible with a claim that some inputs genuinely work; they must not be substituted for its universal negation.

## What remains open

The audit has completed coverage of the present records, not empirical verification or an exhaustive opposition corpus. The most consequential research tasks are independently valid outcome comparisons, detection of a relevant modifiable opportunity, prospective input selection, causal effects, mechanism discrimination, response frequency and durability. Broader outcomes and mediation have additional burdens. The philosophical and practical principles also remain open to reasoned disagreement.

The working account retains its declared premises and equal-priority grounded profile. No priority, protected axiom, extra effect premise or reassuring answer has been added to force a preferred conclusion. Formal opposition is evaluated whenever explicitly admitted to a theory; questions and hypothetical runs do not silently enter the working theory. This distinction is part of the completed schema contract, not a promise of a completed evidence base.
