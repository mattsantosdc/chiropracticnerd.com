## Motor strategy

A motor strategy is a coordinated pattern of neuromuscular organization used to stabilize, move, protect, explore, or otherwise meet a demand. Regulation is distributed across spinal, brainstem, cerebellar, cortical, sensory, and biomechanical processes.

## Not neural reductionism

Bones, joints, connective tissue, injury, development, strength, fatigue, gravity, and environment constrain the strategies available. Saying that tone and movement are regulated outputs does not mean the nervous system acts independently of the body. The claim is interactional: nervous system, body, task, and environment jointly shape what is observed.
